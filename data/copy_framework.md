# Kortexa carousel copy framework

Adapted from the owner's copywriting course (direct-response copy: five elements,
lead with the problem, emotion first then logic, AIDA, one call to action).
Every carousel follows this. Writers: read data/brand.json and data/content_bank.md too.

## The five elements (decide these BEFORE writing any slide)
1. **Target customer**: specific enough that they think "that's me".
   e.g. "Agency owners in East Legon", "Agents who post listings on WhatsApp Status",
   "Developers selling off-plan to diaspora buyers".
2. **Desired outcome**: the specific thing they want. Not "more sales": "more DMs on the
   listings you already have", "viewings booked from Instagram", "a listing video that
   looks premium".
3. **Benefit**: an extra on top of the outcome. "without filming", "without paying for more
   ads", "using the photos you already have", "in under 15 seconds".
4. **Pain point**: the worst part of chasing the outcome. "posts that get 3 likes and no
   messages", "editing videos yourself at night", "buyers who go quiet after 4 hours".
5. **Signature solution**: a named method that sets us apart. Helpful posts point to
   **The Listing Enquiry Playbook** (free; Comment LISTING) or a named piece of it:
   "the 15-second listing video", "the 5-line caption", "the order that sells".
   Offer posts point to the done-for-you premium listing videos or the free listing review.

## Principles
- **Lead with the problem.** Make them feel it ("stab, then twist"), then show the fix.
  The better we name the problem, the better it works.
- **Find the deeper problem.** Symptoms ("my posts get no DMs") come from a deeper cause
  ("the listing isn't packaged to be noticed"). Naming the deeper cause makes us the
  people who understand them.
- **Emotion first, then logic.** Hook with the feeling, then give the reasons and steps.
- **Think like a prosecutor, not a writer.** What must they believe, in what order,
  before they'll act? Each slide moves them one belief further.
- **Repeat the core promise.** Say the outcome more than once in different words.
- **Talk to "you".** One-to-one. The speaker is "I" (the founder).
- **Throw rocks at the common enemy**: photo dumps, "DM for price", hype marketers,
  slow replies, boosting bad posts. Never attack competitors by name, never agents themselves.
- **Proven structure over cleverness.** Clear beats creative.
- **Every carousel ends with ONE action.** Judge it by how many take that action.

## Carousel structure (8–10 slides; never more than 10)
| Slide | Job (AIDA) | What goes on it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | Attention | Call out the target + desired outcome + pain in one bold line. Pattern interrupt. e.g. "YOUR LISTING ISN'T BAD. IT'S INVISIBLE." |
| 2 The real problem | Interest | Name the deeper problem / the limiting belief: "Most agents think they need more listings. You don't." |
| 3 What it costs | Interest | What the belief leads to, in their life: posts ignored, viewings lost, weekends editing. Validate how they feel. |
| 4 "It doesn't have to be that way" | Desire | The solution as a METHOD, not our product: "All you need is a listing that's packaged to be noticed." |
| 5–8 The steps | Desire + logic | One step or idea per slide, from the playbook. Concrete, specific, local. |
| 9 Takeaway (optional) | Desire | One line they'll remember, or a quick checklist. |
| Last CTA | Action | ONE action. Default: "Comment LISTING and I'll send you the playbook." |

Shorter variants are fine (7 slides) for list posts; keep slide 1, at least one problem
slide, the steps, and the CTA.

## Post types and how the structure bends
- **Mistakes & fixes**: hook names the symptom; one mistake + fix per slide.
- **How-to**: hook promises the outcome + benefit; steps are numbered.
- **Before & after**: slide 1 hook, then BEFORE (what most agents post) vs AFTER (premium
  version), explain each change. Uses real photos only when the owner provides them.
- **Speed & replies**: dramatise the buyer's side (they messaged 3 agents), then the fix.
- **AI explained / myth-busting**: state the myth plainly, then the honest truth. No hype.
  Be honest about what AI does (turns real photos into smooth video) and doesn't do
  (it shouldn't invent rooms, furniture or views).
- **Agency growth**: diaspora buyers, consistency, five places.
- **Offer (about 1 in 10)**: full structure, then a slide introducing the done-for-you
  premium listing videos ("all done for you, all you do is send the photos"), then
  "DM me" or the free listing review. Never prices. Value framing: "one extra rental a
  month covers it". No guarantees, no testimonials until real ones exist.

## Slide writing rules
- Headings: Montserrat ExtraBold, UPPERCASE, max ~7 words. Wrap 1–2 key words in
  **double asterisks** for lime highlight.
- Body: max ~30 words, short sentences, eighth-grade English.
- One idea per slide. No walls of text.
- No prices, no made-up numbers, no guarantees, no "made with AI" labels.

## Caption formula (mirrors the playbook's 5-line idea)
1. Hook line (restate slide 1 in different words).
2. 2–4 short lines of the key takeaway.
3. A question to spark comments, OR the CTA.
4. CTA line: "Comment LISTING and I'll send you the playbook." (same keyword every time)
5. 3–5 hashtags (Instagram reportedly caps posts at 5): e.g. #ghanarealestate #accrarealestate
   #realestateghana #eastlegon #realestateagent
