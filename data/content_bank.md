## Kortexa's own first carousel (draft, already written)
1. The biggest problem in real estate isn't finding leads. It's losing them.
2. Every day, real estate agents lose deals they could have closed. Not because they're bad at sales. Because they can't be everywhere at once.
3. An interested prospect asks for property details. You get busy. You forget to follow up. The opportunity disappears.
4. The problem isn't a lack of leads. The problem is that manual processes can't keep up with growth.
5. Imagine a system that: responds instantly, qualifies buyers automatically, follows up without fail, books viewings while you focus on closing deals.
6. That's why we created Kortexa. To help real estate professionals respond faster, follow up consistently, and never let opportunities slip through the cracks.
7. Every missed message means a missed commission.
8. Kortexa: helping real estate agents close more deals with intelligent automation.

## What the system does (use for explanations, not hard selling)
- Replies to leads instantly on the website and social media (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp-style chats).
- Qualifies buyers (budget, location, timeline) before the agent spends time on them.
- Books viewings and can handle payments.
- Follows up with leads who went quiet.
- Hands the conversation to a human agent when it matters.
- Uses the agency's own listings and FAQs so answers are accurate.
