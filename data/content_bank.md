## The Listing Enquiry Playbook (the free PDF; CTA: "Comment LISTING")
Promise: How to turn the listings you already have into posts and videos that make buyers stop
scrolling and send you a message. 15-minute read, made for agencies in Ghana, costs nothing extra.

### Why most listings get no messages
"You don't need more listings. You need the ones you have to get noticed."
01 The photo dump: twenty photos in random order, with the gate or a blurry corridor first.
   Buyers decide in about a second whether to keep looking.
02 No hook: nothing in the first moment says why this home is worth their time (area, size, standout feature).
03 Hidden basics: no price, no location, no bedroom count. People who have to ask for basics often don't bother.
04 No clear next step: "DM for more info" is weak. Buyers need one simple action, like a keyword or a WhatsApp link.
05 Posted once, in one place: one Instagram post, then silence. Most of your audience never sees it.
"The fix isn't more money on ads. It's packaging the same listing so it gets noticed, understood and acted on."

### Step 1: Shoot photos that sell the home (a recent phone works)
- Shoot in daylight with curtains open and lights on. Late morning usually gives the most even light.
- Clear the clutter: buckets, wires, clothes, anything personal.
- Shoot from the corner of each room at chest height, so the room looks open and true to size.
- Hold the phone straight. Tilted walls make a home look cheap.
- Take both shapes: upright for Reels, Status and TikTok; sideways for property websites.
- Never edit the layout. Make it look good, keep it honest. Buyers who feel misled don't come back.
- The order that sells: 1 best exterior or "hero" shot, 2 living room, 3 kitchen, 4 master bedroom,
  5 master bathroom, 6 other bedrooms, 7 standout feature (pool, view, garden), 8 neighbourhood or nearby landmark.
- Tip: always lead with the single most impressive shot, even if it's not the front. That shot is your hook.

### Step 2: The 15-second listing video
Short videos reach far more people than photo posts on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp Status.
- 0–3 sec HOOK: your best shot with bold text on screen, like "4-bed with pool, East Legon."
- 3–12 sec THE TOUR: four or five of your best spaces in the Step 1 order. Smooth, slow camera moves.
  Each shot two to three seconds, never longer.
- 12–15 sec THE ASK: price, location and one clear action: "DM TOUR to book a viewing."
- Make it look premium: bright warm lighting (dark rooms look small and unloved); captions in your brand
  colours, big enough to read with the sound off; music that fits (calm for luxury, upbeat for apartments);
  nothing fake (no added furniture, rooms or views).
- No time to film? AI can now turn your existing listing photos into smooth, cinematic videos,
  without a camera crew or a reshoot.

### Step 3: The caption that gets people to message (same five lines every time)
1. Hook: the standout feature and area in one line.
2. Basics: bedrooms, bathrooms, for sale or rent.
3. Three highlights: the three things buyers in this area care about most.
4. Price and location: show the price. Listings with a price filter out time-wasters.
5. One action: one keyword or one WhatsApp link. Never three options.
Before: "Beautiful house for sale. Very nice and spacious. DM for more info and price. #realestate #ghana"
After: "Pool, garden and quiet street in East Legon. / 4 bedrooms · 4 bathrooms · for sale /
✓ Gated, 24-hour security ✓ Fitted kitchen ✓ 5 minutes to the main road / [Price] · East Legon, Accra /
DM "TOUR" to book a viewing."
Tip: use a different keyword for each listing (TOUR1, TOUR2) so you know which property each message is about.

### Step 4: One listing, five places
Instagram (Reel plus a photo carousel), TikTok (the same 15-second video), Facebook (Page post and local groups),
WhatsApp (Status and broadcast lists), property sites (sideways photos and full details).
- Post consistently. A few good posts every week beats ten in one day and then nothing for a month.
- Repost strong listings. Most of your followers missed it the first time.
- Show the agency, not just houses. A short clip of you or your team builds trust that photos can't.
- Reply fast, or lose them: more enquiries only matter if someone answers. A buyer who waits hours for a
  reply has usually messaged three other agents by then. Decide now who replies, and how fast.

### Step 5: Track what works
A simple count per listing: posted on, enquiries, viewings, deal? After a month you'll see which style of post brings real buyers.
Pre-posting checklist: hero shot first · daylight, no clutter · upright and sideways photos ·
15-second video (hook, tour, ask) · captions readable with sound off · price and location shown ·
three highlights in the caption · one keyword or WhatsApp link · posted in all five places · someone ready to reply fast.
